Enter a team Competitions
Who can enter?

Anyone aged 16 or over on the first day of play. Under-18s need a parent or guardian to sign a consent form at check-in. No residency requirement.

How many players on a squad?

Football 5 to 8, basketball 3 to 4, volleyball 6 to 10, badminton doubles 2, table tennis and the 100m sprint 1. Trim before your first match, never after.

Can I play in more than one competition?

Yes, provided you are not on two squads inside the same competition. Submit one form per competition and mention it in the notes.

When do entries close?

TBC.

What does it cost?

TBC per competition. Solo events are priced lower than team events, and the fee is collected at check-in, not through this site.

What should we bring?

Your kit, shin pads for football, non-marking indoor shoes for the hall. Balls, nets, bibs and water are provided.

What if it rains?

Outdoor matches pause and resume when it is safe. If a full day is lost, affected brackets move indoors or shift to a reserve date, TBC.

Can we get a refund?

TBC. Withdrawals before the draw is published are normally refunded. After that it depends on whether your slot can be filled.

Can spectators come?

Yes, free. Seating at the main arena, standing room at the outdoor pitches.

Who do we ask on the day?

The check-in desk at the main entrance. Before the event, email hello@example.com.
