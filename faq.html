Register Competitions
Who can enter?

Students from any college. Bring your college ID and roll number to check-in. Under-18s need a parent or guardian to sign a consent form at check-in.

Why do you need my roll number and course?

To check you are a student, to keep the draw sheets sorted, and so the check-in desk can find you by roll number on the day.

Do I need an email address?

No. The form asks for a contact number and nothing else. We confirm your entries on that number, and put draws up on this site.

What is the specialisation field for?

Courses like B.Com often carry one, such as CA. If yours does not, leave it at None.

Can I enter more than one event?

Yes. Tick every event on the same form, one entry each. Just check the times on the programme page first, because you cannot be in two events at once.

Why do you ask for height and weight?

Arm wrestling is bracketed by weight, and height seeds the throw-in. Both are read once from the form and are not published anywhere.

Is Throw In only for girls?

Yes, that is the one restricted event. Every other event, including arm wrestling, is open to all entrants.

How do football and basketball entries work?

Both are played as teams, but you enter as an individual. Give the same team name in the notes on each player form, or enter once and list the squad in the notes. Squads are 7 to 10 for football and 3 to 4 for basketball.

TBC.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Entry is free for every event, and no payment is taken anywhere on this site or at the desk.

What should I bring?

Your ID, indoor shoes for the hall, shin pads for football. Balls, nets, boards and water are provided.

What if it rains?

Outdoor events pause and resume when it is safe. If a full day is lost, affected draws move indoors or shift to a reserve date, TBC.

Can I change my events after registering?

Write to hello@example.com before entries close and say which events to add or drop. Once the draw is published, changes depend on whether the slot can be filled.

Can spectators come?

Yes, free. Seating at the main arena, standing room at the outdoor pitches.

Who do I ask on the day?

The check-in desk at the main entrance. Before the event, email hello@example.com.
