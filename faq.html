Rules and FAQ

The short version.

Everything below applies to every competition unless a sport page says otherwise. Anything marked TBC is being confirmed.

Who can enter?

Anyone aged 16 or over on the first day of play. Under-18s need a parent or guardian to sign the consent form at check-in. There is no residency requirement.

How many players can be on a squad?

It depends on the sport: 5 to 8 for football, 3 to 4 for basketball, 6 to 10 for volleyball, 2 for badminton doubles, 1 for table tennis and the 100m sprint. Squads can be trimmed before your first match, never after.

Can I play in more than one competition?

Yes, as long as you are not registered on two squads inside the same competition. Submit one form per competition and tell us in the notes so we can keep your slots apart.

When do entries close?

TBC.

How much is the entry fee?

TBC per competition. Fees are collected at check-in, not through this site. Solo events are priced lower than team events.

What equipment do we need to bring?

Balls, nets and match equipment are provided. Bring your own kit, shin pads for football, and non-marking indoor shoes for the hall courts. Bibs are available if your team has no matching shirts.

What happens if it rains?

Outdoor matches pause and resume when it is safe. If a full day is lost, affected brackets move indoors or shift to the reserve date, TBC.

Can we get a refund?

TBC. Withdrawals before the draw is published are generally refunded; after that, refunds depend on whether your slot can be filled.

Can spectators come?

Yes, entry for spectators is free. There is seating at the main arena and standing room at the outdoor pitches.

Who do I contact on the day?

The check-in desk at the main entrance, or email hello@example.com before the event.
