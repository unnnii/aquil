On the day

Check-in
Thirty minutes before your first match, at the main entrance, with your roll number.
Bring
College ID, indoor shoes for the hall, shin pads for football.
Provided
Balls, nets, boards, bibs and water. Changing rooms are shared.
Late entries
Forfeit the first slot. Tell us in advance and we will try to move you.

Draw and results

The draw goes up the evening before and is posted at check-in. Results are written up after each round on the board by the main arena.
