On the day

Check-in
Thirty minutes before your first match, at the main entrance.
Bring
Photo ID, your full squad, shin pads for football, indoor shoes for the hall.
Provided
Balls, nets, bibs and water. Changing rooms are shared.
Late teams
Forfeit the first slot. Tell us in advance and we will try to move you.

Draw and results

The draw goes up the evening before and is posted at check-in. Results are written up after each round on the board by the main arena.
