On the day

What to expect.

Arrive early

Parking and the check-in desk open 45 minutes before the first whistle. Changing rooms are shared.

Bring your kit

Shin pads for football, non-marking shoes for indoor courts. Balls and nets are provided.

Stay for the final

Presentation is straight after the last final on Day 2. Trophies for winners, runners-up medals for the rest.
