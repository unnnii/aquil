Entry includes

Officials
A referee or umpire on every match.
Equipment
Boards, boards men, balls, nets and bats provided.
Facilities
Changing rooms, water and a first-aid desk.
Format
Every entrant gets at least one match, every event.

How to enter

Individual events take one form per person, and you can tick several events on the same submission.

Carroms, badminton, football 7-a-side and basketball 3x3 take a separate team form, one submission per team.
