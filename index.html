How it works

Three steps from sign-up to first whistle.

Choose your competition

Each sport has its own bracket, squad size and entry fee. Single-entry events are marked on the competitions page.

Register your team

One form per team, no account needed. You get an email confirmation, then we send the draw and check-in details.

Check in on the day

Arrive 30 minutes before your first match with your full squad. Draws go up the evening before.

Schedule

Two days, back-to-back brackets.

Group stages run in the morning, knockouts in the afternoon. The timetable is provisional until entries close.
