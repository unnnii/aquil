How entries work

Registration is per person, not per team. One form covers every event you are entering, so tick all of them at once.

Football 7-a-side and basketball 3x3 are still played as teams. Enter as an individual and name your team in the notes, or enter everyone separately with the same team name.

Students from any college can enter. Bring your college ID and roll number to check-in.

Weight and height

Arm wrestling is bracketed by weight, and the throw-in uses height as a seeding figure. Both are read once from the entry form and never published.

Throw In is the only event with a restriction: it is open to girls only. Everything else is open to everyone.

Not listed

If enough people ask before entries close, we will add an event. Write to hello@example.com with the sport and how many people could take part.
