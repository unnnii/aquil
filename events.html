How entries work

Individual events — chess, table tennis, arm wrestling, penalty shootout and the throw in — take one form per person. Tick every event you are entering on that single submission.

Carroms, badminton, football 7-a-side and basketball 3x3 are team events and take the team form: one submission per team, with the captain's details and the squad listed. Carroms and badminton are pairs, football is 7 to 10, basketball 3 to 4.

Students from any college can enter. Bring your college ID and roll number to check-in.

Weight and height

Arm wrestling is bracketed by weight, and the throw-in uses height as a seeding figure, so the individual form asks for both.

Throw In is the only event with a restriction: it is open to girls only. Everything else is open to everyone.

Not listed

If enough people ask before entries close, we will add an event. Write to hello@example.com with the sport and how many people could take part.
