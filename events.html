Points distribution

Individual events are entered by your batch captain: one form per batch, listing everyone and the event they are playing. Ask your captain to put your name down, and check the list before your first round. Team events are entered by the team captain.

Placings earn points for the batch: first to fourth score, and a dash means no points for that place.

Points awarded per placing, by event.
Event Type 1st 2nd 3rd & 4th
Chess Individual 50 30 20
Table Tennis Individual 50 30 20
Arm Wrestling Individual 20 10 5
Penalty Shootout Individual 70 – –
Throw In (Girls) Individual 70 – –
Carroms Double 75 50 30
Badminton Group 150 100 75
Football 7-a-side Group 150 100 75
Basketball 3x3 Group 100 75 50

Penalty Shootout and Throw In also pay 3 points for every participation. A team that does not report for an event takes 15 negative points.
