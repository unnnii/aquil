Points distribution

Individual events are entered by your batch captain: one form per batch, listing everyone and the event they are playing. Team events are entered by the team captain.

Placings earn points for the batch: first to fourth score, and a dash means no points for that place.

Penalty Shootout and Throw In also pay 3 points for every participation. A team that does not report for an event takes 15 negative points.

General instructions

These hold for every event, whatever the sport.

  1. A student can take part in at most 3 group events and 2 individual events.
  2. Every team must name a team captain.
  3. Arm wrestling registration is on 25 November 2026 (Wednesday). Candidates are weighed on the spot in Room 414 as part of registering.
  4. No changes are allowed after registration, except in a medical emergency.
  5. For a substitution, the team captain must inform the Technical Committee at least one hour before the event starts.
  6. Play in good spirit. Serious disciplinary action follows any violation.
  7. The decision of the referee is final.
  8. Chess is an open event. Every other event runs separately for men and women.
  9. Women can register on the spot for Throw In on 27 November 2026 (Friday) and the Penalty Shootout on 30 November 2026 (Monday), during the semifinals and finals.
  10. A team that fails to report for an event, a no-show, takes 15 negative points.
  11. Report to the venue at least 10 minutes before the scheduled start. A 5 minute grace period after the start is allowed.
  12. Anyone still missing at the end of the grace period is a no-show, and the opposing team is awarded the winning points.

Badminton
Format
Group
Date
23 November onwards
Time
1:45 pm
Venue
Badminton court, 3rd floor
  • The game runs separately for men and women, in a league format.
  • At least two participants per category — two girls and two boys — must register. There is no maximum.
  • After each team has played two matches, the team with the fewest points on the leaderboard is eliminated.
  • After the third round the top four teams go through to the semifinals.
  • Each team brings its own rackets.
  • Every tie is a singles and a doubles match. If the tie is level, a reverse singles decides it.
  • Report to the venue 15 minutes before the scheduled time.

Football 7-a-side
Format
Group
Date
26 & 27 November 2026
Time
1:45 pm
Venue
SH School Ground
  • A league in two pools, Pool A and Pool B.
  • One team per batch, of 10 players: seven on the field and three substitutes.
  • Only two sports-quota players can play in a match at a time.
  • After two matches, the team with the fewest points in each pool is eliminated.
  • After the third round the top two teams from each pool go through to the semifinals.
  • A win is 3 points, a draw 1, a loss 0.
  • Goals scored, goals conceded and the goal difference decide the qualifiers.
  • Games last 7 + 7 minutes with 3 minutes added in the qualifiers and pre-quarterfinals, and 10 + 10 minutes with 4 minutes added in the semifinals and final.
  • Report to the venue 15 minutes before the scheduled time.
